# Inbox

Dump zone. Anything at all: voice-note transcripts, photos of whiteboards, a paragraph
you typed at midnight, a forwarded email, a business card scan.

**Rules**
- No structure required. Filename doesn't matter.
- Run `/process-inbox` to have it read, filed into the brain, and cleared.
- Gitignored — safe for rough or sensitive material, but still no passwords or card numbers.
