# Template: Complaint / escalation

**Use for:** service failures where a first attempt was already made.

```
Subject: Escalation — <issue> — ref <number> — unresolved since <date>

Dear <name / Sir or Madam>,

I raised <issue> on <date> under reference <number>. <What was promised> was promised
by <date>. As of today, <what has actually happened — factual, no adjectives>.

Timeline:
- <date> — <event>
- <date> — <event>
- <date> — <event>

I am asking for <the specific remedy>, by <date>.

If this is not resolved by then, I will <next step: ombudsman / consumer forum /
cancellation>. I would much rather settle it with you directly.

<Sign-off>
<Name> · <Contact> · <Account ref>
```

**Rules**
- The timeline does the work. Facts with dates, nothing emotional.
- One specific remedy, one deadline, one stated consequence.
- Stay polite. Politeness plus a dated timeline is what gets escalated internally.
