# Template: Family letter

**Use for:** letters to parents, relatives, a long-distance sibling, a child.
This is the one template that should sound *least* like a template — treat the structure
as a prompt list, not a form.

```
<Greeting as actually used at home — "Dear Papa", "Priya di", etc.>

<Open with them, not with you. Something specific you remember from the last
conversation: their health, their trip, their worry.>

<What's actually happening in my life — one honest thing, not a status report.
Include a detail, not a summary. Detail is what makes a letter feel like a person.>

<Something for them specifically: an answer to something they asked, a photo
referenced, news they'd care about, a small thing I noticed that reminded me of them.>

<What's next — when we'll speak, when I'll visit, what I'll send.>

<Close warmly. Ask about someone else in the family by name.>

<Sign-off as used at home>
```

**Rules**
- Never let Claude invent family news. Ask what to include, use only what's given.
- Check `../../people/family/` before drafting — names, health, sore subjects.
- Match the language: if letters home are in another language or a mix, write it that way.
