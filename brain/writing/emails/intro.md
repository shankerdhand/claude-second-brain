# Template: Cold intro / reaching out

```
Subject: <specific — mention the shared thing, not "Introduction">

Hi <name>,

<One line on how I found them / who connected us — the credibility line.>

<One line on what I do, framed around what they'd care about.>

<The ask. Make it small and specific: 20 minutes, one question, one intro.>

<Optional: one line of proof — a result, a client, a link.>

<Sign-off>
```

**Rules**
- Three short paragraphs, under 120 words. Long cold emails don't get read.
- The ask must be answerable in one reply.
