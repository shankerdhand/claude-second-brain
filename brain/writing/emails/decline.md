# Template: Saying no

```
Subject: <original subject>

Hi <name>,

Thanks for thinking of me on <thing>.

I'm going to pass — <one honest, short reason. Capacity is a complete reason.>

<If true and you mean it: an alternative — a person, a smaller version, a later date.>

<Sign-off>
```

**Rules**
- Decision in the first two lines. Burying a no reads as a maybe.
- One reason, not three. Three reasons invites negotiation on each.
- Don't offer an alternative you don't want to honour.
