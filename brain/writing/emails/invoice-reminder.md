# Template: Payment reminder

```
Subject: Invoice <number> — due <date>

Hi <name>,

Invoice <number> for <amount>, covering <work>, was due on <date>. I've attached it
again for convenience.

Could you let me know when it's scheduled for payment? If it's stuck with accounts,
point me at the right person and I'll chase it there.

<Sign-off>
```

**Rules**
- Neutral tone until it is 30+ days late. Facts, not friction.
- Always offer the "who else should I talk to" route — usually it's just stuck.
- At 45 days, switch to `../letters/complaint-escalation.md`.
